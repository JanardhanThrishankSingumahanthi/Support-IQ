# SupportIQ Express Replacement Policy
1. Replacement Eligibility:
Customers may request an express replacement within 14 days of receiving damaged or defective equipment.
2. RMA Requirements:
A valid Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number must be generated through the customer support portal.
3. Shipping and Handling:
SupportIQ covers all standard return shipping costs for verified warranty defects.
4. Non-Eligible Items:
Accidental water damage and unauthorized third-party modifications void express replacement eligibility.

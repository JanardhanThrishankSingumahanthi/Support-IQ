# SupportIQ Final Paper Changelog

This document records the exact, section-by-section refinements and academic improvements made during the transition from the first complete draft ([`research/SUPPORTIQ_RESEARCH_PAPER_DRAFT.md`](file:///c:/Users/janardhan-thrishank-Singumahanthi/OneDrive/Desktop/Support%20iq/research/SUPPORTIQ_RESEARCH_PAPER_DRAFT.md)) to the final research paper edition ([`research/SUPPORTIQ_RESEARCH_PAPER_FINAL.md`](file:///c:/Users/janardhan-thrishank-Singumahanthi/OneDrive/Desktop/Support%20iq/research/SUPPORTIQ_RESEARCH_PAPER_FINAL.md)).

| Section | Original Issue | Change Made | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abstract** | Phrasing around hardware trade-offs lacked explicit RRF weight specification and contextual boundary markers. | Added explicit RRF weights ($0.60$ lexical / $0.40$ vector) and inserted contextual boundaries (*"within the evaluated dataset"*, *"under the evaluated experimental conditions"*). | Ensures technical precision and prevents overgeneralization of results beyond the tested holdout benchmark. |
| **Section 1: Introduction** | Minor stylistic informality in enterprise support workflow transitions. | Polished sentence flow, strengthened the framing of unaugmented LLM operational liabilities, and streamlined the three standard RAG vulnerabilities. | Enhances academic rigor and readability for peer review. |
| **Section 4: Dataset** | Paragraph describing total chunks lacked explicit consolidated inventory count. | Added explicit sentence: *"Across all 7 documents, a total of 14 discrete policy chunks are maintained in SQLite (`supportiq.db`)."* | Clarifies dataset scale and indexing boundaries. |
| **Section 6: System Architecture** | Fig. 1 diagram labeled vector stream as "Semantic Channel" and lacked explicit query processing and context assembly stage names. | Updated stage headers to include *Query Processing*, *Context Assembly*, *Grounding Check*, and relabeled the vector stream as *"Deterministic Vector Channel: Cosine similarity over 32-dim hash vectors"*. | Completely eliminates any ambiguity regarding whether vectors are neural embeddings or deterministic hash vectors, and maps stages to the full pipeline specification. |
| **Section 7: Retrieval and RRF** | Section 7.2 subtitle used generic vector terminology. | Updated subtitle to *"7.2 32-Dimensional Deterministic Hash-Vector Representation"* and reinforced the explicit technical disclosure denying learned neural embeddings. | Enforces strict terminology consistency across all retrieval sections. |
| **Section 14: Discussion** | Section 14.1 listed results sequentially without an explicit synthesis of operational trade-offs and lacked a non-generalization caveat. | Expanded Section 14.1 to explicitly contrast FP16 LoRA (lower generation latency: 0.844 s) against 4-bit QLoRA (lower peak VRAM: 0.46 GB, 52.1% reduction), noted identical holdout accuracy/faithfulness (100.0%) on the small dataset, avoided declaring an overall winner, and added an explicit non-generalization caveat. | Complies with academic reviewing guidelines requiring balanced, objective analysis rather than promotional claims. |
| **Section 15: Limitations** | Contained 4 limitation items; lacked explicit disclosure of evaluation scope (single-turn vs multi-turn) and dataset generalization constraints. | Expanded into 6 comprehensive, numbered limitations covering (1) holdout size ($N=10$), (2) hardware boundary (single RTX 2050 laptop GPU), (3) hash-vector vs continuous embedding limits, (4) policy document domain, (5) single-turn conversational scope, and (6) generalization constraints. | Satisfies rigorous peer-review standards of transparency and empirical boundary setting. |
| **Section 16: Conclusion** | Phrasing in the concluding paragraph could be read as generalizing beyond the tested hardware and benchmark. | Refined conclusion to focus strictly on evidence-based findings (*"on the evaluated frozen holdout benchmark"*, *"under the evaluated experimental conditions"*), highlighting the trade-offs between LoRA and QLoRA. | Eliminates any potential overclaiming while delivering a concise, strong summary. |
| **Section 17: Future Work** | Needed clearer distinction between future directions and existing capabilities. | Numbered and formally articulated 5 specific future extensions (learned neural bi-encoders, expanded multi-turn benchmarks, broader document domains, CRM ticketing APIs, and human-in-the-loop DPO). | Distinguishes future research from completed implementation. |
